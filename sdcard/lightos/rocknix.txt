ROCKNIX boot flag, read by boot.ini. The card boots Lume at every power-on.
When the first line is "R" and a token that is new each time the flag is
armed, the next power-on boots ROCKNIX once for that token. U-Boot keeps
the token it booted in sector 31 of this partition, so the power-on after
that is Lume again. Arm it with tools/rocknix_once.sh from the computer.
Holding VOL- at power-on boots ROCKNIX too, without the flag.
