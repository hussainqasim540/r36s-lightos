R36S LightOS boot flag, read by boot.ini. The first line is "L" and a token
that is new each time the flag is armed: the next power-on boots LightOS
(lightos/initrd.img) once for that token. U-Boot keeps the token it booted
in sector 31 of this partition, so the power-on after that is ROCKNIX again.
Arm it with tools/lightos_once.sh from the computer, or with "LightOS boot
once" in ROCKNIX's Ports.
